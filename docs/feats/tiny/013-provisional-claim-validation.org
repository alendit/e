#+title: Provisional Claim Validation

* Change

Keep a provisional Bayesian answer visible while its corrective follow-up runs.
Show the capability-provided =Validating claims…= activity in its place, and
hide the provisional answer only after the replacement reply has completed.

The harness carries optional pending hook activity without interpreting the
capability's policy.  The chat shell renders it generically.

* Validation

- Bayesian hook tests cover delayed supersession.
- Chat tests cover the pending activity and ordinary progress rendering.
