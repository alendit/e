* Architecture review

The plan places session/participant durability with the session application
service, child execution admission with the subagent runner, Daily retry and
continuation policy with Grimoire, and rendering with presentation shells.
Dependencies remain directed from Grimoire policy and shells toward existing
domain-owned e contracts.
No durable replica, global ordering service, or synchronous persistence
boundary is introduced.

The four delivery packages are useful stopping points and explicitly remove
the false-running and update-session legacy paths.
Uncertain retry counts and presentation details remain local policy choices;
stable lifecycle meanings and SQLite authority are direct contracts.
