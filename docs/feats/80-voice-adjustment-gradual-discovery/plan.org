* Status

Done.

* Goal

Keep passive avoidance of recently corrected writing tells cheap while making
the active voice-check and learning workflow explicitly on demand.

* Contract

- The capability contributes no dedicated model-facing voice tools.
- Record, list, and clear remain capability actions callable through
  =e-actions-call=.
- Default stable context contains only a short pointer to an on-demand guide.
- Cached tell labels may enter dynamic context as one compact avoidance line;
  detailed descriptions stay in =e://voice-adjustment/tells=, and mutable tells
  never claim stable-prefix placement.
- The full check, rewrite, and learning workflow lives at
  =e://voice-adjustment/skills/voice-adjustment= and says not to run an extra
  check unless explicitly requested.

* Validation

Focused tests cover tool absence, action round trips, guide and tells resources,
and the exact compact default-context shape.  Compile and the complete ERT suite
form the repository gate.
