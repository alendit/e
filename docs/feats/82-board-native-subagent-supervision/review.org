* Findings

** TODO High: Durable terminal reports fail when no manifest fact is present

*** Expected

Slice 6 requires a structured child report to publish its durable terminal fact before local settlement, and it requires report lookup to work from persisted child-session assignment data rather than the live registry.
The focused tests added in =ec45b4de= exercise that direct report path with a spawned child carrying =run-1/review/0= assignment metadata.

*** Observed

Both =e-subagent-runner-test-durable-report-precedes-local-settlement= and =e-subagent-runner-test-durable-assignment-survives-registry-loss= fail in the complete suite with =e-board-orchestration-invalid-fact :manifest missing=.
=e-board-orchestration-actions--notify-projection= advises every successful =e-board-orchestration-publish-fact= call and immediately requests a run projection.
For a terminal-report fact with no manifest on that board, =e-board-orchestration-run-projection= invokes the reducer, and the reducer rejects the absent manifest.

*** Why this blocks acceptance

The durable report publication path throws instead of publishing the report and preserving the local child outcome.
It breaks both the report-before-settlement contract and restart-independent report lookup covered by slice 6.

*** Smallest corrective direction

Make the projection notification path tolerate an orphan or not-yet-visible report without changing fact publication semantics, or require and validate the manifest before a child may receive durable assignment metadata.
Retain the direct-report tests and add coverage for the selected policy.

*** Evidence

The failures occur in =test/e-subagent-runner-test.el:801= and =test/e-subagent-runner-test.el:829= during =eldev test=.
The failing call path is =e-board-orchestration-actions--notify-projection= at =lisp/layers/agents/e-board-orchestration-actions.el:219= through =e-board-orchestration-run-projection=.

** TODO High: The final end-to-end fixtures from slice 10 are absent

*** Expected

Slice 10 requires one restart fixture that dispatches required and optional work, restores the board journal and task queue, accepts one report per task, and emits exactly one reconciliation input.
It also requires one long-operation fixture that crosses multiple supervision windows without automatic cancellation.

*** Observed

The new orchestration tests cover isolated reducer, queue, continuation, and view cases.

No test names or exercises a long operation crossing multiple supervision windows.

*** Why this blocks acceptance

The feature's two stopping points are live supervision without durable orchestration and durable orchestration after restart without a parent await.
The final gate does not yet prove either complete workflow as the plan requires.

*** Smallest corrective direction

Add the two slice-10 fixtures before re-running the full suite.
Keep them batch-only and deterministic; they do not require a live Emacs reload.
