* New findings

** P1 F88.9: Exact lifetime projection omits accepted live static/stable context

*** Evidence

The plan describes exactly three model-visible lifetimes and says context
reconstruction is exactly =checkpoint + durable tail + open frame=.  Accepted
Features 23 and 35 and current =e-capabilities=/=e-harness= also rebuild and
prepend live =static-prefix= and =stable-context= segments on every request.
Those segments must remain live-configurable rather than being dropped, frozen
into a generation, or left as an unowned fourth path.

*** Owning correction

Scope the three lifetimes to session-derived causal history and define the full
semantic request as:

#+begin_example
active static-prefix + active stable-context
+ generation checkpoint + durable tail + open observation frame
#+end_example

=e-capabilities= continues rebuilding the first two segments from the active
capability set.  They are not copied into generations or promotions.  Their
fingerprints participate in anchor compatibility, so live changes appear on the
next request and rebuild incompatible anchors without compaction.  Current-state
segments move to the observation frontier.

Acceptance must change static instruction and stable provider content
mid-generation, prove replacement and anchor invalidation, and prove the
generation/checkpoint did not change.  Fingerprint/layout tests are mechanism
evidence.

** P1 F88.10: Settled tool calls have no restart-safe outcome contract

*** Evidence

The corrected transaction settles tool calls before execution and claims exact
single dispatch, but two durable states remain ambiguous after a crash:

1. acknowledged admission followed by exit before invocation; and
2. irreversible side effect followed by exit before durable result.

Both replay as call-without-result.  Retrying may duplicate case 2; skipping
loses case 1.  Arbitrary tools cannot promise exactly-once external side effects
without tool-owned idempotency.

*** Owning correction

Give every accepted ordinary tool call a stable execution id and append-only
execution states separate from frame settlement: admitted, dispatch-started,
and terminal finished/failed/outcome-unknown.  Checkpoints retain nonterminal
obligations.  Acknowledge dispatch-started before invocation and terminal result
before the next tool or provider follow-up.

An admitted-but-undispatched call may resume.  A dispatch-started call without a
terminal result becomes =outcome-unknown= and is not automatically retried unless
the tool explicitly supplies a stable idempotency key or resumable contract.
Promise one live dispatch per execution attempt, not impossible exactly-once
effects across arbitrary crashes.

Required contract evidence includes:

- restart before dispatch resumes one previously undispatched call;
- restart after controlled side effect but before result yields
  =outcome-unknown= and zero automatic redispatch;
- idempotent retry reuses its execution/idempotency key;
- completed multi-tool results remain terminal and only provably undispatched
  calls can start; and
- provider follow-up waits until every admitted call is terminal or explicitly
  resolved.

A restart E2E with a controlled side-effecting tool is contract evidence.
Exact execution-record/checkpoint encoding tests are mechanism evidence.
