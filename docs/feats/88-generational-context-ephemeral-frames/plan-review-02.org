* New findings

** P1 F88.6: Request-time current state has no restart-stable observation representation

*** Evidence

Current canvas/attachment context is rebuilt from mutable live buffers or files
at request time.  The plan says a frame stores observation entry ids, persists
them before dispatch, and retains referenced records across checkpoints, but
does not say how a volatile canvas snapshot obtains stable identity or what a
retry does if its source changes or disappears.

*** Owning correction

Every admitted observation needs a restart-stable provider-neutral reference.
A journal-backed observation references its exact payload entry.  A request-time
observation records provider owner, durable source reference, source version or
content fingerprint, render-layout revision, and rendered fingerprint; mutable
buffer objects are not durable references.

Retry rebuilds request-time state and compares fingerprints.  A match retries
the same frame.  A mismatch or unavailable source appends =frame-aborted= and
opens a newly captured frame; it never substitutes new bytes into the old frame
or marks the old observation consumed.

Acceptance must interrupt an open canvas frame, mutate or remove its source,
restart, and prove explicit abort/new-frame behavior with no stale inherited
marker.  Descriptor serialization and checkpoint-retention tests are mechanism
evidence.

** P1 F88.7: Tool side effects are not ordered after admission and settlement

*** Evidence

Current =e-loop= can enqueue and execute a streamed tool-call item before the
provider response completes.  The plan validates promotion before mutation and
makes ordinary calls durable transition records, but does not require the
complete promotion/tool-call set to be validated and settled before dispatch.
An invalid promotion emitted beside a tool call could therefore cause an
irreversible side effect before the transition is admissible or durable.

*** Owning correction

Buffer reserved effects and ordinary tool calls until response completion.
Validate the complete set before dispatch.  Persist and acknowledge the
consuming response, accepted promotions, and =frame-settled= before executing
ordinary tools.  Validation or persistence failure dispatches no tool and
leaves the frame retryable.  Persist completed tool results with the next
=frame-opened= before any provider follow-up.

Required contract partitions:

- invalid one-over promotion plus ordinary tool call dispatches zero tools;
- withheld or failed settlement acknowledgement dispatches zero tools; and
- successful acknowledgement dispatches each admitted tool once and opens one
  next frame containing its results.

Exact serialized record-order tests are mechanism evidence.

** P2 F88.8: Durable acknowledgement cost has no metric or acceptance evidence

*** Evidence

The plan inserts a durability wait between response completion and observation
exclusion, anchor advancement, provider follow-up, or tool dispatch.  Existing
performance acceptance covers token/cache behavior only.  Asynchronous I/O
protects redisplay but does not prove bounded transition latency or work.

*** Owning correction

For observation-bearing and multi-tool transitions, report settlement-prefix
record count and bytes, acknowledgement wait, and time from provider completion
to the next admitted tool/provider dispatch.  Work must be proportional to the
settling prefix, never scan the full journal, and perform no synchronous
filesystem I/O on the Emacs main thread.

A controlled-writer acknowledgement gate is contract evidence; queue/outbox
microtests are mechanism evidence.
