* Findings

** TODO P1-06: =F88A4-DURABLE= does not define the literal portable message produced by a v3 item

*** Contract cell

=F88A4-DURABLE= at its =F88A3-SEM= and =F88A3-LAYOUT= composition.

*** Observed source fact

Addendum 4 permits heterogeneous independently rendered source values, treats a
tool source as bounded result content rather than its replay envelope, stores
=:value= for exact items and =:text= for summaries, and says only that
projection renders the value or text (=addendum4.org:90-100,177-208=).  It does
not define a portable role, content mapping, prefix, or serialization rule.

Current projection requires role-bearing portable messages and permits only
system, user, and assistant roles
(=lisp/core/e-context-lifetime.el:39-46,277-304=).  V2 promotion projection
chooses system authority and adds a =Promoted fact ID:= wrapper
(=lisp/core/e-context-lifetime.el:910-927=).  Tool-result content has no portable
durable role of its own.

*** Consequence

Implementations could preserve a transient role, elevate to system, demote to
user, stringify structured content, retain the obsolete prefix, or emit a
roleless item.  Those alternatives change authority, literal request bytes,
stable-prefix behavior, and compaction output.

*** Minimal correction

Projection must map each v3 item, in record order, to exactly one portable
message:

#+begin_src emacs-lisp
exact item   -> (:role system :content EXACT-CANONICAL-SOURCE-VALUE)
summary item -> (:role system :content MODEL-AUTHORED-TEXT)
#+end_src

Core inserts stored content without labels, estimates, IDs, a =Promoted fact=
prefix, or =prin1= conversion.  Content must already be accepted by the
portable-message codec.  Compaction must consume the identical role/content
sequence.  V2 retains its existing projection solely for compatibility.

*** Acceptance evidence

- exact string, structured content, and summary each produce one literal system
  message;
- exact content is equal before decoration and after projection;
- no presentation/provenance/prefix/stringification leaks;
- item order is keep order followed by summary order;
- compaction preserves the sequence; and
- v2 rendering remains unchanged.

** TODO P2-07: Slice 2 commits v3 records before Slice 3 establishes their codec

*** Contract cell

Implementation ordering for =F88A4-ATOMIC= and =F88A4-DURABLE=.

*** Observed source fact

Slice 2 commits the prepared record before frame consumption
(=addendum4.org:313-317=), while Slice 3 introduces its codec and projection
(=addendum4.org:319-324=).  The session append boundary always normalizes a
context record through the promotion decoder before mutation and currently
accepts only v2 (=lisp/core/e-session.el:3655-3676,3728-3756=).

*** Consequence

Slice 2 cannot be accepted without duplicating or bypassing Slice 3 work, and a
revision could write a record it cannot reopen.

*** Minimal correction

Make Slice 2 establish =F88A4-DURABLE= first: portable mapping, strict v3 codec,
append/replay/projection, read-only v2 dispatch, and mixed-version reopen,
branch, and compaction.  It stops at a tested session boundary accepting a pure
prepared v3 record with no provider writer.  Make Slice 3 establish
=F88A4-ATOMIC= and the =F88A3-TOOL=/=F88A3-SEM= joins using that boundary,
including carrier, acknowledgement, live-frame preparation, append, consume,
and composed observe-curate-forget behavior.

*** Acceptance evidence

- Slice 2 append/reopen works without a carrier or bypass;
- Slice 3 uses the accepted append/codec surface;
- no revision writes an unreplayable durable record; and
- each later slice depends only on accepted earlier behavior.
