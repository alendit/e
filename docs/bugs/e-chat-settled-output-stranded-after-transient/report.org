#+title: e-chat Settled Output Stranded After Transient Collapse
#+startup: overview

* Report

After a long agent turn finishes, the chat transcript can leave the final
assistant answer around the middle of the output pane with a large empty area
below it.  The composer remains at the bottom and the turn reports =done=, but
the transcript no longer presents the latest output at its intended bottom
position.

The reported Grimoire daily turn had 63 tool calls before settlement, making
the live activity region substantially taller than the final answer and
activity summary that replaced it.

Expected behavior: a transcript that was following live output before the
terminal render continues to follow the settled answer.  A transcript that the
user explicitly scrolled upward remains at that scrollback position.
