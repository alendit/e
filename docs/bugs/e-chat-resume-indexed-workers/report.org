#+title: Resume picker lists indexed worker sessions as top-level chats
#+startup: overview

* Report

Opening the switch-session picker after restarting Emacs showed durable
subagent sessions alongside ordinary top-level chats.  The visible entries
included plan workers and daily-helper prompts such as =Work in ...=,
=Complete daily/archive/...=, and =Run exactly ...=.

These worker sessions have their own dedicated surfaces and must remain
directly addressable, but they do not belong in general chat resume, switch,
active-session, or overview pickers.

After the initial cold-loader fix, running =e-dev-reload= reproduced the same
polluted picker.  A full live reload must apply corrected index interpretation
to the retained session store, not require an Emacs process restart.
