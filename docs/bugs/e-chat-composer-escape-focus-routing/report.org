#+title: e-chat Escape Leaves Keystrokes in the Composer
#+startup: overview

* Report

In a composed chat, pressing Escape appears to focus the transcript, but Enter
and =d= are still handled by the composer.  Enter does not activate the focused
turn and =d= invokes composer editing instead of opening turn details.

One Escape should transfer keyboard ownership from the composer pane to
response navigation in the transcript pane.
